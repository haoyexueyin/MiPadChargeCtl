# Authors and development disclosure

The project owner directed the work, supplied hardware and firmware evidence, chose the
operating policy, and performed every real-device test.

The initial driver, controller, scripts, tests, safety analysis, and documentation were
developed with **OpenAI Codex** through an iterative human-in-the-loop engineering process.

Codex-generated changes were compiled, statically checked, independently reviewed for
high-risk paths, and validated against the project owner's Xiaomi Mi Pad 2. This disclosure
describes the development process; it does not transfer responsibility for installing or
operating experimental kernel software.
